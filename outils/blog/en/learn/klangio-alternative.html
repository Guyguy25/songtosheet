Klangio alternative: Tablurio vs Klangio

Two tools that turn a song into sheet music or tabs with AI. Here is what really sets them apart, so you can pick the one that suits you.

By Tanguy Bois · 6 min read · 25 November 2026

  2. Side-by-side comparison
  3. When to pick Klangio
  4. When to pick Tablurio

In short

Klangio is a Berlin company with a family of transcription apps, one per instrument: Guitar2Tabs, Piano2Notes, Sing2Notes, Drum2Notes and more, plus a multi-instrument “Transcription Studio”. Tablurio brings guitar, piano and vocals together in a single web app, and also accepts Spotify and Deezer links.

Both do the core job well: you give them a song, the AI writes the notes. The differences are in the sources, the instruments, how you try them and the pricing model.

When to pick Klangio

  • You want to transcribe drums or other instruments Tablurio doesn’t offer.
  • You want to record yourself directly in a mobile app.
  • You need a native Guitar Pro export.
  • You work on multi-instrument songs with their Transcription Studio.

When to pick Tablurio

  • Your song is on Spotify or Deezer: paste the link and Tablurio finds the song.
  • You play several instruments and want everything in one app, without a subscription per instrument.
  • You want to compare versions: several engines transcribe the song and Tablurio recommends the closest one.
  • You’d rather pay once (Lifetime).
  • You want to practise with synced, slowed-down audio playback.

Best option: try both on your song

Transcription quality depends a lot on the song: the mix, which instrument stands out, the playing style. Both tools have a free trial. Test the same passage on each and keep the one that gets it closest.

For Tablurio, paste your link into the guitar tab generator or the piano sheet music tool.

FAQ

Is Tablurio free like Klangio?

Both have a free trial: 3 previews of 30 seconds on Tablurio, a demo of the first 20 seconds on Klangio. Full songs and exports are paid on both.

Which one is more accurate?

It depends on the song. Both use AI and can get a very busy passage wrong. The most reliable way is to test the same excerpt on both.

Does Tablurio accept YouTube links like Klangio?

Yes, as well as Spotify and Deezer links and audio files.

Can I open the result in Guitar Pro?

With Tablurio, yes, through the MusicXML export. Klangio also offers a native Guitar Pro export.
