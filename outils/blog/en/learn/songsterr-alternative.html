Songsterr alternative: Songsterr or Tablurio?

Songsterr and Tablurio don’t do the same job. One gives you a tab that already exists, the other writes the one that doesn’t exist yet.

By Tanguy Bois · 5 min read · 25 November 2026

Two different approaches

Songsterr is first and foremost a library: over a million licensed guitar, bass and drum tabs, with a player that plays the tab in time. If your song is there, it’s often the best option.

Tablurio is a generator: paste a YouTube, Spotify or Deezer link, or upload an audio file, and the AI writes the tab or sheet music of that exact recording. Handy when the tab doesn’t exist, or for a cover, a live version, your own recording.

When to pick Songsterr

  • Your song is in its catalogue: you get a checked tab and a great player.
  • You play drums.
  • You want to browse lots of well-known songs without generating anything.

When to pick Tablurio

  • The tab doesn’t exist: a rare or recent song, a cover, a live version.
  • You have the song on Spotify, Deezer or YouTube and want its transcription directly.
  • You want piano or vocal sheet music, not just a tab.
  • You want to transcribe your own recording.
  • You want a MIDI or MusicXML file to edit.

The winning combo

Many guitarists use both: Songsterr for the classics in its catalogue, and a generator for everything else. Look for your song on Songsterr first; if it isn’t there, paste its link into the guitar tab generator.

FAQ

Does Tablurio replace Songsterr?

Not really: they complement each other. Songsterr offers existing, checked tabs; Tablurio generates the ones that don’t exist.

Is Tablurio free?

You get 3 free 30-second previews. Full songs and exports come with the Starter, Pro and Lifetime plans.

Are generated tabs as accurate as Songsterr’s?

A hand-checked tab is still more reliable than an automatic transcription. The generated tab gets the vast majority of notes right, but a busy passage may need a touch-up.

Does Tablurio do drum sheet music?

No, Tablurio covers guitar, piano and vocals, with bass in beta.
