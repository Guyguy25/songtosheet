The guide

Deezer, YouTube and the 30-second preview

The full audio of Deezer tracks is protected. Tablurio uses your link to identify the song, then looks for the same song on YouTube. You confirm the version found (or an alternative) before the transcription starts.

If the song isn’t on YouTube, Tablurio can use Deezer’s official 30-second preview when it’s available. For the full song, paste a YouTube link or upload your audio file.

Which Deezer links work?

  • Track links (deezer.com/track/… or share links): yes.
  • Playlists, albums and Flow: no, pick a specific track.

FAQ

Does Tablurio download audio from Deezer?

No. The link is used to identify the song; Tablurio finds the same song on YouTube. Otherwise, Deezer’s official 30-second preview can be used.

Can I transcribe a full song from Deezer?

Yes, as soon as the YouTube version is found. Otherwise, paste a YouTube link or upload your audio file.

Can I paste an album or playlist link?

No, paste the link of a specific track.

Is it free?

You get 3 free previews of 30 seconds. Full songs and exports come with the Starter, Pro and Lifetime plans.

Which export formats?

PDF to read or print, MIDI and MusicXML to edit in your software.
