The guide

Tab or standard notation?

Tablature is the guitarist’s favourite notation: each of the six lines is a string, and each number tells you which fret to play. You don’t need to read music to use it, which is why self-taught players love it.

Standard notation gives the exact pitch and rhythm of every note on a staff. It’s more precise for rhythm and easy to share with other musicians.

On Tablurio you don’t have to choose once and for all: for guitar, you ask for a tab or sheet music depending on what you prefer to read.

How to read the generated tab

  • The top line is the high E string, the bottom line the low E string.
  • A 0 means open string, a 3 means 3rd fret.
  • Numbers stacked vertically are played together (a chord).

Which songs?

Riffs, arpeggios, melodies, acoustic intros: transcription is most reliable when the guitar or melody stands out in the mix. On very busy songs some passages may need a touch-up, which you can do in Guitar Pro or MuseScore thanks to the MusicXML export.

A tab maker that does the writing for you

Most online tab makers and tab creators give you an empty grid and let you type every number yourself. Tablurio works the other way round: you bring the song, and it writes the tab. It’s handy for a guitar riff you want to learn, an intro, a solo or a whole song, and you can then fine-tune the result in Guitar Pro or MuseScore.

Can I use it as a guitar riff maker?

Yes. Upload a recording of a riff, or paste the link of a song and pick the excerpt you want: Tablurio writes the tab of that passage.

Is the tab generator free?

You get 3 free previews of 30 seconds to try it on your own songs. Full songs and exports come with Starter (€10/month), Pro (€19/month) and Lifetime (€99 once).

Can I get standard notation instead of a tab?

Yes. For guitar, you choose between tab and standard notation.

Which tuning is used?

Tabs are written for a 6-string guitar in standard tuning (E A D G B E).

Can I open the tab in Guitar Pro or MuseScore?

Yes, export it to MusicXML, the exchange format supported by most notation software, including MuseScore and Guitar Pro.

Does it work with my own recording?

Yes. Upload your MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC file (25 MB max) instead of pasting a link.
