The guide

What is Tablurio?

Sheet music is usually sold one song at a time, and many songs simply don’t have any. Tablurio starts from the music instead. Its AI analyses the audio, detects pitches and rhythm, and writes them as a tab or on a staff, depending on the instrument you picked.

Several transcription engines each produce a version, and Tablurio recommends the one closest to the song. You get synced audio playback with adjustable speed, so you can practise a passage slowly before playing it at full tempo.

What can you transcribe?

  • Guitar: guitar tabs or standard notation, for riffs, intros, solos or whole songs.
  • Piano: piano sheet music on two staves.
  • Vocals: the sung melody on a staff.

Where can the song come from?

  • A YouTube video, including Shorts.
  • A Spotify or Deezer track: Tablurio identifies the song and finds it on YouTube.
  • Your own audio file: MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC.

Exports: PDF to print, MIDI for your music software, and MusicXML to edit in MuseScore, Guitar Pro, Dorico or Sibelius.

It’s an automatic transcription: it gets most notes right, but a very busy passage may need a touch-up. Only transcribe songs you’re allowed to, for example for your own practice.

FAQ

Answers before you play.

Another question? See the full FAQ or pricing (in euros).

Is Tablurio free?

Your free account includes 3 previews of 30 seconds to judge the result on your own songs. Full songs and exports come with Starter (€10/month), Pro (€19/month) or Lifetime (€99 once).

Which instruments are supported?

Guitar (tab or standard notation), piano and vocals. Bass tabs are in beta.

Which links work?

YouTube videos and Shorts, Spotify and Deezer tracks. You can also upload an MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC file.

How long does a transcription take?

Usually thirty seconds to a minute, depending on the length of the excerpt.

How accurate is it?

It gets the vast majority of notes right on songs where the instrument is clearly audible. Very dense mixes may need a touch-up, which the MusicXML export makes easy.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. The whole app, from generation to your account, is in English. Your choice is remembered with the EN / FR switch at the top of the page.
