The guide

What is a MIDI file for?

A MIDI file contains no sound: it contains instructions (which note, when, how long, how loud). Any virtual instrument or keyboard can then play them with whatever sound you like.

What you can do with it

  • Learn piano with an app that shows falling notes, like Synthesia.
  • Produce: drop the MIDI into GarageBand, Logic Pro, Ableton Live or FL Studio and change the sound, tempo or key.
  • Play it on your digital piano or synth.
  • Fix notes in a piano roll before importing everything into notation software.

MIDI or MusicXML?

MIDI is made for sound and production. If you want to edit the written score (bars, tab, layout), go for MusicXML. Good news: Tablurio plans include both, plus PDF.
