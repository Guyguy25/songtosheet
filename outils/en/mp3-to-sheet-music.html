The guide

Why convert a file rather than a link?

Not every song is on YouTube. Your own compositions, a lesson recording, your band’s demo or a file bought from an online store: file upload lets you transcribe exactly the audio you have.

Supported formats

  • MP3: the most common, perfect for most uses.
  • WAV and FLAC: lossless, ideal for demos and quality recordings.
  • M4A: the format of iPhone voice memos and many downloads.

Maximum size: 25 MB per file, and 10 minutes of audio per generation.

Prepare your recording

The cleaner the instrument, the better the result: avoid background noise, record close to the source, and trim long silences. A voice memo of you humming a melody can also become vocal sheet music.

Only upload recordings you have the right to transcribe (your compositions, your own practice…).

FAQ

Answers before you play.

Note: the Tablurio app is in French for now. See pricing (in euros).

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A and FLAC, up to 25 MB.

Is there a length limit?

Yes, 10 minutes of audio per generation.

Can I convert a voice memo from my phone?

Yes. iPhone voice memos are M4A, a supported format. Choose “Vocals” to get the vocal line as sheet music.

Is the conversion free?

You get 3 free previews of 30 seconds. Full songs and exports come with the Starter, Pro and Lifetime plans.

Can I convert MP3 to MIDI?

Yes: once the sheet music is generated, export it to MIDI with a paid plan.
