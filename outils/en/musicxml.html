The guide

What is MusicXML?

MusicXML is an open format designed to exchange sheet music between programs. Unlike a PDF (a fixed image) or MIDI (played notes, no layout), it describes the score itself: notes, rhythms, bars, clefs, accidentals, and even strings and frets for a tab.

Why export it?

  • Fix a note or rhythm after the automatic transcription.
  • Transpose the song to a key that suits your voice or instrument.
  • Arrange: add fingerings, dynamics, a second part.
  • Share the score with a teacher or your band, who’ll open it in their own software.

MusicXML, MIDI or PDF?

PDF is for reading and printing. MIDI is for playback and sound. MusicXML is for editing the score. With a Tablurio plan, you get all three.

Which software opens MusicXML?

Most score editors: MuseScore (free), Dorico, Sibelius, Guitar Pro, and many mobile apps.

What’s the difference between MusicXML and MIDI?

MIDI describes played notes (pitch, duration, velocity), great for sound. MusicXML describes the written score (bars, clefs, tabs), great for notation.

Is MusicXML export included in the free plan?
