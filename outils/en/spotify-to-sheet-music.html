The guide

Why Tablurio goes through YouTube

Spotify doesn’t let anyone retrieve the audio of its tracks. Instead of blocking you, Tablurio uses the Spotify link to identify the song (title, artist), then looks for the same song on YouTube. You see the version found before the transcription starts, and can choose another one if it’s not right (live, remix, cover…).

If the song can’t be found, paste a YouTube link directly or upload your own audio file.

Which Spotify links work?

  • Track links (open.spotify.com/track/…): yes.
  • Playlists, albums and podcasts: no, pick a specific track.

FAQ

Answers before you play.

Note: the Tablurio app is in French for now. See pricing (in euros).

Does Tablurio download audio from Spotify?

No. The Spotify link is used to identify the song; Tablurio then finds the same song on YouTube and suggests the version to use.

What if the suggested version is wrong?

Pick one of the alternatives, or paste the YouTube link of the version you want.

Can I paste a playlist link?

No, paste the link of a specific track.

Is it free?

You get 3 free previews of 30 seconds. Full songs and exports come with the Starter, Pro and Lifetime plans.

Which instruments are available?

Guitar (sheet music or tab), piano and vocals. Bass is in beta; violin and drums are coming soon.
