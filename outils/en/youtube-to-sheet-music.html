The guide

Why start from YouTube?

YouTube is the largest free music library in the world: studio versions, live shows, covers, acoustic takes, songs you can’t find anywhere else. The sheet music, on the other hand, often doesn’t exist… or is sold one song at a time.

With Tablurio, the video becomes the source of your sheet music. The AI analyses the audio, detects pitches and rhythm, then writes them on a staff or a tab depending on the instrument you picked.

Which videos work best?

  • Clean sound: a studio version or a good live recording beats a phone recording.
  • A clear lead instrument: solo piano, acoustic guitar or upfront vocals make the transcription easier.
  • Up to 10 minutes: longer links are refused, but you can pick the excerpt you want.

On very dense arrangements (orchestras, walls of distorted guitars), a few passages may need a touch-up. That’s exactly what the MIDI and MusicXML exports are for: fix it in a few clicks in your favourite software.

Only use Tablurio for songs you’re allowed to transcribe, for example for your own practice. You confirm it with every generation.

FAQ

Answers before you play.

Note: the Tablurio app is in French for now. See pricing (in euros).

Does Tablurio work with YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Regular video links (youtube.com/watch), short links (youtu.be) and Shorts are all accepted.

How long does it take?

Usually around thirty seconds to a minute, depending on the length of the excerpt and server load.

Is it free?

Your free account includes 3 previews of 30 seconds to judge the result on your own songs. Full songs and exports come with the Starter, Pro and Lifetime plans.

What is the maximum video length?

10 minutes of audio per generation. For longer songs, pick the excerpt you want to practise.

Is the sheet music 100% accurate?

No, it’s an automatic transcription. It gets the vast majority of notes right, but a very busy passage may need a touch-up. Always check the result before playing.

Is Tablurio available in English?

These pages are in English, but the Tablurio app itself is in French for now. The steps are simple: paste a link, pick an instrument, get your notes.
