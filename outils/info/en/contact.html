What to write to us about

Help and sheet music

A generation that didn’t go as expected, a question about a result or an export format.

Your plan

You cancel or change plan yourself from “My plan”. For a withdrawal or a payment issue, write to us.

Rights and reports

If you’re a rights holder and some content is a problem, tell us: it’s removed quickly after checking.

To help us help you faster

  • The email address of your Tablurio account.
  • The song link or the name of the file.
